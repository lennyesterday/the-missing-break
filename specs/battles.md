# Third feature: the rhyme book, the rap battle, the DJ battle and the endings

## Why

The crew has to win "Think About It" back in a rap battle and then play the DJ battle at Disco Fever. The game needs two reachable endings, one of them depending on what the player did before (PT05, PT R4, A5).

## What

- **The rhyme book** (`hq`): clicking the book on the couch opens its sign with **TAKE IT**. Taking it collects `rhyme_book` (once, PT R2/R3); a "YOU GOT THE RHYME BOOK!" banner shows, the couch is empty, and from then on MC Bible holds his book in every picture and walk (`has-rhyme_book` / `lacks-rhyme_book`).
- **The rap battle** (`station`), as graffiti buttons in the picture:
  - **WHO GOES FIRST?** The player picks who raps first: click Meyhem or Bible (a ring pulses round each), or the **MEYHEM FIRST ▶** / **BIBLE FIRST ▶** buttons. The crew hints at the right order ("I'LL GO FIRST." / "AND I'LL FINISH HIM."). The order is remembered (`GameState.rap_battle`, saved): `None` → `meyhem_went_first` → `won`, or `lost`.
  - **Meyhem first** (right): Meyhem walks to the mic and raps while his verse plays (`static/audio/mc_meyhem_verse.mp3`); Krete gets a "?!". Then **BIBLE, FINISH HIM ▶** (or click Bible): needs `rhyme_book` (without it Bible goes up anyway and **chokes**, see below). With it Bible walks to the mic and raps (his verse), Krete hands over the record: the crew wins `vinyl` (once).
  - **Bible first** (wrong): with `rhyme_book`: Bible raps his verse, then Meyhem his, but after Bible his bars don't hit as hard: the crowd picks Krete and he keeps the record: **game over** (see below).
  - **Bible without his rhyme book** (first, or after Meyhem): he walks to the mic empty-handed and chokes: he forgets his bars ("UHH...", sweat, scratching his head), the crowd boos (BOOO!, thumbs down) and Krete asks "WHERE'S YOUR BOOK, BIBLE?". His choke is a recording made with Suno (`static/audio/mc_bible_chokes.mp3`, 41 s). Then: **game over**.
  - **Game over** (`ending_lost_battle`, *Krete Keeps the Crown*): when the rapping is over (or skipped), the game-over screen comes: Krete on his crate holding the record up, the Sopranos cheering, the crowd booing, GAME OVER and why (Bible choked / went first). Its only button is **RESTART ↻**: a new game straight away (saved), from the story intro. Like every ending it is saved and shown again by RESUME. A recording that isn't there is left out, so then game over comes straight away.
  - **While someone raps:** he walks from his spot to the mic stand and raps there (his spot is empty, the mic is off the stand, the audience throws its arms up). **SKIP ▶▶** (top right) stops the verse (and the next one). When a verse is over, the page says so and the next rapper is up. Leaving the station stops the verse. What happened comes up once the rapping is over. Skipping or listening saves nothing.
- **Disco Fever** (`battle`): the block gets a **DISCO FEVER ▶** button. The club: neon sign, disco ball, moving light spots, a light-up dance floor, the crowd, the DJ booth with a Soprano on the decks, King Krete on a speaker and the host; the crew comes in, the conversation is clicked through, then **START THE BATTLE**.
- **The endings** (PT05): `start_battle` ends the game with
  - `ending_legends` – **Legends of the Bronx** – if the crew has `vinyl`: Needle cuts in "Think About It", Reekz breaks, Bible and Meyhem on the mic, Portals' backdrop pulls the room into another dimension, confetti, the crowd jumps;
  - `ending_static` – **Lost in the Static** – otherwise: grey and static, Reekz stays standing, the crowd shrugs, the Sopranos take the crown.
  
  The ending is saved. Once it is reached, no story actions are offered or accepted. RESUME shows the ending again; **BACK TO THE START** leads to the home screen, where START asks before replacing the game (A6).
- **Songs**: MC Bible's verse (`static/audio/mc_bible_verse.mp3`, made with Suno, see `static/audio/CREDITS.md`) and MC Meyhem's (`static/audio/mc_meyhem_verse.mp3`) play when they rap in the battle (`logic.RAPPERS`, an action's `raps`). Songs are listed in `logic.SONGS`; a thing's `song` and an action's `song` point to one. Every picture has a sound on/off switch (top right), remembered in the browser. Browsers only allow sound after the player has clicked something, so the first sound comes after START/RESUME at the earliest.
- **Saving**: the save also remembers `ending`. Saves from before (without the column) still load.

## Out of scope

The tag decision on the block (`tag_choice`: read / bomb, PT04) and Krete refusing the battle after `bomb`; the MENU articles; songs for the things that can be clicked.

## Done when

1. The rhyme book can be taken once; Bible holds it in every picture afterwards.
2. Meyhem first, then Bible with the rhyme book wins the record once; Bible without the rhyme book chokes, Bible first (with the book) loses: both are game over (saved), and RESTART starts a new game from the intro.
3. Disco Fever is reached from the block; START THE BATTLE ends with Legends of the Bronx with the record and Lost in the Static without it.
4. At an ending no story action can change the game; the ending is saved, RESUME shows it, BACK TO THE START leads home.
5. Both endings are reachable from a new game (the win and lose paths in `docs/story.md`), also in the app.
6. Old saves still load.
- **Plot twist: practice first.** Once the crew has `vinyl`, the basement offers **PRACTICE THE BREAK ▶** (action `practice`, once; `GameState.practiced`, saved; older saves load as not practiced). Needle walks to the sleeve, picks it up, carries it to the end of the DJ table, steps behind it and goes to the decks (drawn behind the table), puts the record on; then the practice minigame, **KEEP THE BREAK GOING** (`assets/screens/practice.svg`), the 70s "merry-go-round": two copies of "Think About It" on two decks seen from above, the break playing on one (its bar runs down), the other cued up. One round on a deck is two loops of the drum break (8.4 s), and the picture runs on the music's clock (`data-sync`), so the decks switch exactly when the loop starts over. SPIN BACK counts only in the green window at the end of the round (the button glows green; the page judges the timing), and the window gets smaller with every clean switch (0.8 s, 0.55 s, 0.35 s). The press shows at once (SPUN BACK ✓ / TOO EARLY ✗) and takes effect on the loop's restart: the other deck takes over and the first spins back (CLEAN!); too early, or no press: THE BREAK DIED!, the count starts again at the easiest window. Three clean switches in a row and he has practiced (only then saved), and the drum break fades out; back in the basement READY FOR THE FINAL! ◀ STOP leaves without it. The drum break of *Think (About It)* (`static/audio/records/think_about_it_break.wav`: cut to start on the first drum hit, 8 times its 2-bar loop of 4.199 s, about 114 BPM) plays meanwhile, on a loop. The DJ battle at Disco Fever: `vinyl` and practiced → `ending_legends`; `vinyl` but not practiced → `ending_out_of_practice` (*Out of Practice*: Needle on the decks with the record, the break skips — SKRRT —, the crowd walks off, the Sopranos take the crown); no `vinyl` → `ending_static`. The hint: Bible's win message ends with "Needle hasn't touched it in days...".
